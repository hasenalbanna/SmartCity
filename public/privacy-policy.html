Ceylon Explorer Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

Ceylon Explorer ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data when you use the Ceylon Explorer mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, authentication identifiers.
  • Profile content: profile details, preferences, and settings.
  • User-generated content: chats, trip plans, comments, and uploaded content.
  • Location data: location you provide or allow for travel-related features.
  • Device and usage data: app diagnostics, crash information, and analytics events.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide core app features such as planning, chat, and personalization.
  • To secure accounts and prevent abuse.
  • To improve app quality and performance.
  • To communicate important service updates.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share data with trusted service providers to operate the app, including cloud infrastructure and authentication services such as Firebase.

4. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit.

5. Data Retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for providing the service, legal compliance, and legitimate business needs.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below. We process verified requests within 30 days unless legal retention is required.

7. Children's Privacy

Ceylon Explorer is not directed to children under 13. If we learn we collected data from a child without valid consent, we will delete it promptly.

8. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries or deletion requests, contact: blkcapxtreme@gmail.com

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
