# Ceylon Explorer — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 17, 2026
**Last Updated:** August 17, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Ceylon Explorer** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of the app.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Information:** Name, email address, profile photo when you register
- **Profile Data:** Display name, bio, and profile preferences
- **Trip Details:** Trip names, ratings, and notes you save

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Location Data:** We collect real-time GPS location data **only when the app is open and you start a drive**. We do not track your location in the background.
- **Voice Data:** Audio from LITA AI voice sessions is processed in real-time and sent to Google Gemini AI for response generation. **We do not store your voice recordings.**
- **Usage Data:** App interactions, screens visited, and features used (for improving the app)
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
- **Firebase Authentication:** Login data via Google Sign-In
- **OpenStreetMap / Nominatim:** Location search queries (subject to OSM's privacy policy)
- **Google Gemini AI:** Conversation text is sent to Google's API. Please review [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. Location Data

Ceylon Explorer collects **precise location data** to provide:
- Real-time GPS navigation during active drives
- Speedometer readings
- Trip distance calculation
- Local place discovery

**You can disable location access at any time** in your device settings. Without location permission, drive tracking and navigation features will not function.

We do **NOT** collect location data in the background when the app is closed.

---

## 5. Voice and AI Conversations

When you use **LITA AI**:
- Your spoken words are converted to text on your device using the Speech-to-Text API
- The text is sent to **Google Gemini AI** to generate a response
- LITA's response is read aloud using Text-to-Speech
- **Conversation history is stored locally on your device only**
- We do not store or analyze your voice recordings on our servers

## 6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do **not sell** your personal data. We share data only in these circumstances:

- **Service Providers:** Firebase (Google), Google Gemini API, OpenStreetMap — necessary for app functionality
- **Legal Requirements:** If required by law or to protect rights and safety
- **Business Transfers:** In the event of a merger or acquisition, users will be notified
