## 2. Target Market Segmentation

### Primary Market: International Tourists
- **Size:** 2.1M arrivals/year (2024)
- **Profile:** 25–45 years old, smartphone-native, willing to pay for convenience
- **Top Origins:** India, UK, Germany, China, France, Australia
- **Behavior:** Heavy mobile app users for travel; rely on Google Maps, TripAdvisor, Booking.com

### Secondary Market: Local Travelers
- **Size:** ~3M Sri Lankans who travel domestically per year
- **Profile:** 18–40 years old, digitally engaged, value-conscious
- **Behavior:** Weekend trips, family outings, local exploration

### Tertiary Market: Travel Professionals
- **Size:** ~5,000 travel agents and tour operators in Sri Lanka
- **Profile:** Need efficient planning tools and client management
- **Opportunity:** B2B licensing and white-label offerings

## 4. SWOT Analysis

### Strengths 💪
- Deep Sri Lanka focus — every feature, place, and data point is Sri Lanka-specific
- LITA AI is a unique, emotionally engaging differentiator no competitor has
- Premium design sets it apart from generic local apps
- Full travel lifecycle covered (plan → navigate → book → share)
- Multi-platform: Android, iOS, and Web

### Weaknesses ⚠️
- New entrant — limited brand recognition initially
- AI features require internet (offline limitation)
- Hotel and guide inventory needs to scale
- Single geography limits total addressable market

### Opportunities 🚀
- Sri Lanka government actively promoting tourism — favorable policy environment
- Growing "digital nomad" tourism trend — remote workers traveling to Sri Lanka
- Regional expansion potential: Maldives, Seychelles, other island nations
- B2B licensing to Sri Lanka Tourism Board
- Partnership with airlines serving Sri Lanka (SriLankan Airlines, IndiGo)

### Threats ⚡
- Google could add Sri Lanka-specific features to Google Maps
- Economic or geopolitical instability in Sri Lanka
- Competition from well-funded global travel apps entering the market
