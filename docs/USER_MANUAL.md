## 🚗 Start Drive — GPS Navigation with LITA

**Start Drive** is one of Ceylon Explorer's most powerful features. It turns your phone into a live GPS navigator with an AI travel companion.

### Step 1: Open Drive Setup
- From the Home screen, tap the animated **"Start Drive"** button

### Step 2: Configure Your Trip
1. **From:** Your current live location (auto-detected)
2. **To:** Type your destination, or tap a **Recent Destination** chip (Colombo, Galle, Kandy, Ella)
3. **Vehicle:** Select your vehicle type:
   - 🚗 Car | 🚌 Bus | 🚂 Train | ✈️ Flight | 🚛 Lorry | 🛵 Tuk-tuk | 🚲 Cycle | 🚶 Walk

### Step 3: Preview Your Route
- A live map will appear showing your calculated route
- Multiple route suggestions may appear — select your preferred one

### Step 4: Start Trip!
- Tap the **"Start Trip"** button
- The screen switches to the live navigation view

### During Your Trip:
| Element | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Vehicle Marker** | Moves in real-time as your phone moves |
| **Speedometer** (top right) | Shows your current speed in km/h |
| **Draggable Minimap** (corner) | A small overview map you can drag anywhere |
| **LITA Button** (bottom) | Talk to your AI companion |
| **Green ✓ Button** (top right) | Save and finish your trip |

## 🤖 LITA — Your AI Travel Companion

LITA is your AI best friend who travels with you. She speaks, listens, and keeps you company on every journey.

### Opening LITA
- Tap the **"Travel with LITA"** button at the bottom of the active trip screen

### First Time
- LITA will greet you: *"Hi, I am LITA. Don't worry, I am right here with you! Tell me your name."*
- She will automatically start listening for your reply
- Say your name — she'll greet you by name and ask where you're going!

### How to Talk to LITA
1. **Tap her icon** to start/stop listening
2. **Speak naturally** — LITA listens for up to 30 seconds
3. When you stop talking, she automatically sends your message to AI
4. She will reply in her warm, friendly voice with pink waves pulsing

### LITA States:
| Animation | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Pink waves** | LITA is speaking |
| **Red waves** | LITA is listening to you |
| **Idle icon** | Ready for you to tap |

### You can also type:
- Use the text field at the bottom to type if voice isn't working

## 💾 Saving Your Trip

1. When ready to finish, tap the **green ✓ button** (top right on the navigation screen)
2. A **Save Trip Details** dialog appears:
   - Enter a **Trip Name** (e.g., "Weekend Drive to Galle") 
   - Give a **Star Rating** (drag the slider: 1–5 ⭐)
3. Tap **Save**
4. You're taken to your **Trip Summary** page

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why is LITA not responding?**
A: Ensure you have an internet connection. LITA requires internet to connect to the AI service. Try tapping her icon to restart listening.

**Q: My location isn't updating during the drive.**
A: Go to your phone's Settings → Location → ensure Ceylon Explorer has "Always On" or "While Using" location permission.

**Q: The microphone isn't working for LITA.**
A: Check that Ceylon Explorer has microphone permission in your phone settings. On Chrome (web), click the 🔒 lock icon in the address bar and enable the microphone.

**Q: Can I use Ceylon Explorer offline?**
A: The Explore and Profile sections work with cached data. Live navigation and LITA AI require an internet connection.
