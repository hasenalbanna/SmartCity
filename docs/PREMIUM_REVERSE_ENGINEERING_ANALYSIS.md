## 4. Feature Gating & Conversion UX Architecture

### 4.1 Top-of-Page Persistent Subscription Trigger (`AppTopUtilities`)
To eliminate discovery friction, a dedicated high-visibility Pro button is embedded in the top utility row on all primary screens (`HomeScreen` and `ExploreScreen`):
- **Free Citizens:** Displayed as an illuminated gold pill with subtle ambient glow:  
  `⚡ UPGRADE TO PRO` • Tooltip: *"Upgrade to Falcon Shield+ Pro ($4.99/mo) • Unlock All Features"*.
- **Subscribed Citizens:** Displayed as a luxury obsidian/gold badge:  
  `👑 PRO ACTIVE` or `🏢 ENTERPRISE` • Tooltip: *"Falcon Shield+ Pro Active • Tap for benefits"*.
- **One-Tap Access:** Tapping directly opens `PremiumStoreModal`.

### 4.2 Contextual Feature Gate Modal (`FeatureUnlockModal`)
Whenever a citizen triggers a gated action (e.g. generating a multi-day AI itinerary, exporting a certified insurance PDF, toggling hands-free voice guardian, or reserving a VIP bus seat), the system intercepts the action:
1. **Identifies Target Feature:** Maps the request to `PremiumFeature` enum.
2. **Evaluates Entitlement:** Checks `SubscriptionService.canAccessFeature(feature)`.
3. **Presents Contextual Unlock Sheet:**
   - Displays glowing lock icon and feature title.
   - **Market Benchmark Card:** Explicitly states what rival apps charge (e.g. *"Citizen Plus charges $19.99/mo for 24/7 voice monitoring"* or *"Forensic audits charge $25 - $50 for certified damage reports"*).
   - **Smart City Value Proposition:** Explains how Falcon Shield+ Pro delivers this feature for just $4.99/mo or via a single micro-pass.
   - **Direct Actions:**
     - `👑 Upgrade to Falcon Shield+ Pro ($4.99/mo)`
     - `⚡ Unlock Single Pass` (e.g. LKR 450 for Insurance Dossier)
     - `Cancel / Continue with Free Tier`

## 6. Financial Projection & Unit Economics

Assuming a conservative **3.5% conversion rate** from free citizens to paid tiers across a city of 100,000 active app users:

- **Free Active Users:** 96,500
- **Falcon Shield+ Pro Subscribers (3.0%):** 3,000 users × $4.99/mo = **$14,970 / month** ($179,640/yr)
- **Business Enterprise Subscribers (0.5%):** 500 local merchants × $19.99/mo = **$9,995 / month** ($119,940/yr)
- **Pay-Per-Use Add-On Revenue (Insurance PDFs & Bus Passes):** ~1,200 micro-transactions/mo × $2.00 avg = **$2,400 / month** ($28,800/yr)
- **Projected Total Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR):** **$328,380 USD (~LKR 102 Million / year)**.

This model funds municipal server infrastructure, private contractor repair SLAs, and continuous AI development while keeping essential public emergency services free for every citizen.
